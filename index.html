How a contract runs

One contract account, from lock to a single settlement.

  1. 01

    Lock create_offer

    Your tokens move into a vault owned by the program, with the strike, the expiry and the premium you ask written into the contract account. Until someone fills it, cancel_unfilled hands the tokens back.

  2. 02

    Fill buy_option

    The buyer pays the premium. 97% lands in your wallet in the same transaction and 3% goes to the service vault. From this moment the terms are fixed and the collateral cannot be withdrawn.

  3. 03

    Exercise exercise

    Any time before expiry the buyer can pay the strike in SOL. You receive it minus 0.1%, and the vault releases the tokens to the buyer.

  4. 04

    Or expire expire

    At expiry anyone can call expire and the vault returns the tokens to you. Exercise and expire race for one settlement: the first to land closes the contract, the other is rejected.

GUST is an options market for the tokens people already hold. The seller names a price for the bag, the buyer pays for the right to it, and a program vault holds the tokens until a single settlement.

What happens when every token on Solana, memes first and then anything with a mint, carries a strike, an expiry and a premium someone will pay today?

Why hold $GPACK

  1. 30%of each day's service fees goes to $GPACK staked in the Revenue Vault, shared by stake and time staked. A new stake counts from the next daily period.
  2. 20%of the same fees buys $GPACK on the open market and burns it. Every buy and burn is on Proof with its Solscan transaction.

The fee base is 3% of filled premium and 0.1% of exercised strike, so the vault grows with the number of tokens that get a strike.
